How to prepare the files

Data file (analyst, weekly)

  1. In GA4, export the same daily report each week as a CSV: a Date column plus the approved metric columns.
  2. Keep one header row and one row per day. Remove total and summary lines. Lines that start with # are skipped automatically.
  3. Dates can be written as 2026-11-20, 20261120 or 11/20/2026. Rates can be written as 0.58 or 58%.
  4. Load the file here and enter the export date.

Goals file (goal owners)

  • Columns: Metric, Basis, Direction, Target, Tolerance, Unit, Owner, Last reviewed, Reason.
  • Metric must match a column header in the data file.
  • Basis is Total or Average. Direction is “Higher is better” or “Lower is better”.
  • Unit is Number, Percent or Seconds. Write percent targets as 60% or 0.6, and the tolerance as 5% or 0.05.

Status rules

  • Higher is better: On track at or above the goal. At risk inside the tolerance band below it. Otherwise Off track.
  • Lower is better: On track at or below the goal. At risk inside the tolerance band above it. Otherwise Off track.
  • A grey status means something must be fixed first. No verdict is shown unless every day in the period has one usable value and the goal is complete.

Known limits

  • The refresh is manual, so data can be out of date between exports. GA4 data can take a day or more to finalize.
  • Average goals use a simple average of the daily values, which can differ slightly from the period rate GA4 reports.
  • The dashboard checks that entries are complete. It cannot tell whether a target or an exported number is the right one.
